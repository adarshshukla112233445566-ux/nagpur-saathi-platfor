# 🇮🇳 Nagpur Saathi

### Report · Track · Build a Better Nagpur

Nagpur Saathi is a modern civic-tech web platform designed to help citizens report, track and explore civic services across Nagpur through a simple and user-friendly digital experience.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 Smart City Home Dashboard
- 🤖 AI Saathi — Smart City Assistant
- 📝 Civic Issue Reporting
- 📊 My Reports & Complaint Tracking
- 🗺️ Smart Map with live civic locations
- 🏙️ Explore Nagpur
- 🔔 City Alerts
- 🚑 Emergency & Citizen Services
- 🚌 Transport information
- 🏥 Hospital information
- 🚦 Traffic information
- 🗑️ Garbage reporting
- 💡 Streetlight reporting
- 🐕 Animal Rescue support

### 🤖 AI Saathi

AI Saathi provides a smart city assistant interface where users can ask about hospitals, transport, potholes, places to visit, injured animals and city alerts.

### 📝 Report an Issue

Citizens can report civic problems by selecting a category such as garbage, pothole, streetlight, road damage, water or drainage.

![Report an Issue](./screenshots/report-issue.png)

---

### 🗺️ Smart Map

The Smart Map provides an interactive Nagpur map with different civic categories such as traffic, hospitals, police, fire, complaints, animal rescue, tourist places and parks.

![Smart Map](./screenshots/smart-map.png)

---

### 🏙️ Explore Nagpur

The Explore section allows users to discover popular places, lakes, parks, heritage locations, food destinations and family attractions across Nagpur.

![Explore Nagpur](./screenshots/explore.png)

---

### 🔔 City Alerts

The City Alerts section displays important traffic, weather, civic and emergency notifications with priority levels.
